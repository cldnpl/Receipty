# Note di rilascio per Google Play

Da incollare in Play Console → Release → "Release notes" (en-US, max 500 caratteri).

## 1.3 (versionCode 2)

```
<en-US>
Smarter receipt scanning: the restaurant or shop name, its address and company details printed at the top of the receipt are no longer picked up as items. Only the products you ordered end up in the list.
</en-US>
```

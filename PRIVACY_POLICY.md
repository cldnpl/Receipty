# Receipty Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Receipty is a bill-splitting calculator made by Claudia Napolitano. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: **Receipty does not collect, send or sell any of your data.** Everything stays on your iPhone.

## What the app stores

- **Bills you split.** The date, the names you type for the people at the table, the amounts, the items and the resulting transfers are saved so you can open them again from Recent bills.
- **Your settings.** Your appearance choice, your currency and whether you have seen the introduction.

All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is not uploaded to any server, and the developer has no access to it.

## Camera and photos

- Receipty asks for camera access only to photograph a receipt. You can also pick a photo from your library instead; in that case the app receives only the photo you choose.
- The text on the receipt is read on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition. The photo is used for that reading and is **not saved** by the app and **not sent** anywhere.
- You can turn camera access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app. You can still enter bills by hand.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No payments: Receipty only calculates who owes whom. Money is exchanged by you, outside the app.

## Names of other people

The names you add for the people at the table stay on your device. Use first names or nicknames if you prefer; the app works the same.

## Deleting your data

Swipe left on a bill in Recent bills (or long-press it) and tap Delete to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

Receipty does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an update to the app, with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support link on the Receipty page of the App Store.

# Receipty Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Receipty is a bill-splitting calculator made by Claudia Napolitano. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: **Receipty does not collect, send or sell any of your data.** Your bills stay on your phone. Receipty is available for iPhone and Android; where the two differ, it is said below.

## What the app stores

- **Bills you split.** The date, the names you type for the people at the table, the amounts, the items and the resulting transfers are saved so you can open them again from Recent bills.
- **Your settings.** Your appearance choice, your currency and whether you have seen the introduction.

All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is not uploaded to any server, and the developer has no access to it.

## Camera and photos

- Receipty asks for camera access only to photograph a receipt. You can also pick a photo from your library instead; in that case the app receives only the photo you choose.
- The text on the receipt is read on your phone: on iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition, on Android with Google's ML Kit text recognition, whose model is built into the app. The photo is used for that reading and is **not saved** by the app and **not sent** anywhere.
- You can turn camera access off at any time in your phone's settings. You can still enter bills by hand.

## Android: technical data sent by ML Kit

On Android, Google's ML Kit library may send Google anonymous technical information about how text recognition runs: the device model and Android version, the app's package name and version, and performance figures such as how long a recognition took. It never includes your photos, the text of your receipts, your bills or the names you type. Google uses it to measure and improve ML Kit, it is encrypted in transit and it is not linked to you. See Google's [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

Nothing like this happens on iPhone.

## What the app does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- No third-party SDKs that collect personal data. The only third-party library that talks to the network is ML Kit on Android, as described above.
- No payments: Receipty only calculates who owes whom. Money is exchanged by you, outside the app.

## Names of other people

The names you add for the people at the table stay on your device. Use first names or nicknames if you prefer; the app works the same.

## Deleting your data

Swipe left on a bill in Recent bills (or long-press it) and tap Delete to remove it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

Receipty does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an update to the app, with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support link on the Receipty page of the App Store or Google Play.

## What WhoPays is

WhoPays is a calculator. It helps a group work out how to split a bill and who should pay whom. It does not move money, hold money, process payments or offer financial, tax or legal advice.

## Check the numbers

- Receipt scanning uses automatic text recognition, which can misread names or prices. The app highlights entries it is unsure about, but **you are responsible for checking the items, totals and amounts** before relying on the result.
- Results depend on what you enter: who was at the table, who had what and who paid how much.
- Amounts are rounded to the smallest unit of the chosen currency, so an individual share can differ by one cent (or one unit) from an exact division.
